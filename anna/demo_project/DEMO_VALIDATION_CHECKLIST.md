# Manual validation checklist

- Start ANNA 1.0.0.
- Open `washing_hands.json` from the `demo_project` folder.
- Confirm that the two included videos load correctly.
- Play the first video and confirm that the configured timed pause is presented.
- Confirm that the configured hotspot and supports appear at the timed pause.
- Activate the hotspot and confirm its configured output.
- Confirm that Support 1 displays its text and support image.
- Switch between Professional and User modes and confirm that the project remains available.
- If Research is being validated, start a session, execute the interaction, finish the session, and confirm that the corresponding session output is generated.
